## Why Follow
Thorsten combines deep systems/compiler literacy with an unusually bold, product-first view of how coding agents change software engineering. His weekly Substack (*Register Spill*) mixes concrete workflow protocols from building Amp (Monday single-meeting alignment, "Boxes" / Jellyware, agentic team velocity) with essays on taste, curiosity, and shipping.

## Signature Mental Models & Themes
- **Tokens as a new computing paradigm:** Moving past human-centric code formatting/ceremony toward system composition and business problem-solving.
- **Fast feedback loops & fearlessness:** Investing in sub-second test/deploy loops and diving into unfamiliar codebases without hesitation.
- **"Does it help you ship?":** Evaluating tools, abstractions, and automation by their maintenance tax vs. real user delivery.
- **Jellyware / Boxes:** Running agents directly alongside personal and internal web software on simple VMs without heavyweight container ceremony.
