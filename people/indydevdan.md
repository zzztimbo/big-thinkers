## Why Follow
Where AICodeKing focuses on testing models and tools head-to-head, IndyDevDan focuses on **owning and programming your agent harness**—moving from *human in the loop* (babysitting terminals) to *human near the loop* (durable proactive agents + small specialized teams queuing decisions) to *out of the loop* (event/cron-triggered workflows gated by cheap System-1 classifiers like Jev).

## Signature Mental Models & Themes
- **The Core Four (`Context, Model, Prompt, Tools`):** Model capability is commoditized; your leverage comes from engineering the harness around it.
- **In-Loop -> Near-Loop -> Out-Loop:** Proactive durable agents run ahead of you on schedules/events and pause only at human decision gates.
- **Self-Compacting Agents & System-1 Gating:** Giving agents explicit tools to compact their own context at natural boundaries, and placing ultra-cheap classifiers (Jev) in front of frontier models.
