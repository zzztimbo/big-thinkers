## Why Follow
Mario built the **Pi coding agent** from scratch and writes some of the most honest, hype-free technical postmortems in the space (*What I learned building an opinionated and minimal coding agent*, *MCP vs CLI: Benchmarking Tools for Coding Agents*, *Prompts are code, .json/.md files are state*). He frequently debates Armin Ronacher in public, sharpening both sides' arguments.

## Signature Mental Models & Themes
- **Minimal, opinionated harnesses:** Keeping the core agent loop tiny, inspectable, and bash-first rather than piling on opaque abstractions.
- **Prompts are code; `.json`/`.md` files are state:** Treating LLM workflows with real software engineering discipline and explicit file-backed state.
- **Measure before adopting protocols:** Running controlled cost/time/success benchmarks (e.g., `terminalcp` CLI vs. MCP) instead of arguing from vibes.
