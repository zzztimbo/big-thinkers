## Why Follow
Armin writes from the trenches of designing both classic developer libraries (Python/Rust) and modern agent harnesses (Pi 1.0, Gondolin sandboxes, Codemode). He constantly stress-tests popular assumptions—explaining *why* bash/CLI beats bloated tool schemas, *when* Codemode (harness-side sandboxed JS composition) earns its keep, and how to think clearly about AI's societal tradeoffs (*AI and Irontown*).

## Signature Mental Models & Themes
- **Brains vs. Hands (Harness vs. Execution Environment):** Separating the trusted harness host from the sandboxed target environment where bash/code runs.
- **Code Is All You Need + Codemode:** Using bash in the execution environment and lightweight JS composition (`Codemode`) on the harness side instead of stuffing dozens of raw MCP tools into context.
- **API ergonomics & sharp edges:** Designing libraries and CLIs around how developers (and now RL-trained models) actually compose operations.
