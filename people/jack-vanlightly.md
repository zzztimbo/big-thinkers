## Why Follow
Jack writes some of the clearest deep-dives in the industry on **distributed logs, cloud-native Kafka (Kora / Freight Clusters on S3), Apache Iceberg / table formats, stream processing, and formal modeling (TLA+ / Fizzbee)**. His work pairs directly with Chris Riccomini's storage/streaming writing and grounds high-level data platform decisions in actual protocol trade-offs.

## Signature Mental Models & Themes
- **Virtual Consensus & Composable Durability (`AtomicLog` + `LogDrive`):** Separating failure-free steady-state ordering from fault-tolerant reconfiguration and passive S3/object-store durability.
- **Reliable Progress = Durable Trigger + Progressable Work:** Unifying queues, stream processors, workflows, and durable agents under one reliability equation (idempotency + checkpointed state + replayable triggers).
- **Lightweight Formal Modeling (Fizzbee / TLA+):** Exploring concurrency state spaces and invariants before writing complex distributed code.
